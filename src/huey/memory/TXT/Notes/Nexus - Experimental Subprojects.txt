Deploying legacy Google Nexus devices as raw physical control surfaces for HueyOS requires aggressively stripping down their software layers and physically bypassing their aging power delivery systems.

Here is the technical breakdown for isolating and re-purposing these devices while breathing new life into old tech.

## Hardware Modifications & Power Delivery

* **Battery Elimination:** Old lithium-ion cells pose a significant swelling and fire hazard in a continuously docked state. You can bypass the battery entirely by stripping a USB cable and soldering a 5V source to a step-down buck converter (or TP4056 module), delivering a stable 4.2V directly to the motherboard's battery terminal pins. For devices like the Nexus 5 (`hammerhead`), adding inline capacitors helps handle occasional 2A CPU load spikes.
* **Storage Degradation Bypass:** The 2012 Nexus 7 (`grouper`/`tilapia`) suffers from notoriously slow eMMC due to an early lack of TRIM support and severe NAND degradation over time. The most robust workaround for a permanent terminal node is to bypass the internal storage entirely by running the operating system from RAM (initramfs) or utilizing a fast USB-OTG drive.

## Operating System Pathways

* **Native Mainline Linux:** The postmarketOS community maintains robust mainline kernel ports for these specific architectures. The Nexus 5 (`hammerhead`) and Nexus 7 2012 (`grouper` - Nvidia Tegra 3) both hold active testing and community mainline status.
* **Bootloader Constraints:** The `grouper` (2012 Nexus 7) requires flashing a custom U-Boot implementation to properly boot postmarketOS, as the stock Asus bootloader is insufficient.
* **Fallback Environment:** If native Debian drivers prove unstable (e.g., DRM graphical driver failures on the `flo` Snapdragon S4 Pro), flashing a minimal build of LineageOS coupled with Termux or Dropbear provides a perfectly isolated SSH client environment without the heavy Android GUI overhead.

## Architecture & Integration Setup

By treating these devices strictly as dumb terminals with zero canonical memory, you reinforce a distributed-load architecture that keeps the heavy lifting centralized.

| Layer | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| **Transport** | Hardwired USB tethering to the central Lenovo Legion Go node. |
| **Authentication** | SSH via mTLS (mutual TLS) using ed25519 keys to eliminate password overhead. |
| **Interface** | Python 3.13 `ncurses` dashboards or standard `tmux` sessions triggering modular HueyOS commands. |
