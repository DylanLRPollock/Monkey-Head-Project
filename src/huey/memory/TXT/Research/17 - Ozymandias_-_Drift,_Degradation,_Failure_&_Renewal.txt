## Introduction — What Remains When the Monument Weathers

A machine can operate after the reasons for trusting it have disappeared. Its fans may run, its interface may answer, and its familiar name may appear at startup, even while the signing keys have been compromised, private archives improperly copied, judicial votes forged, or historical state silently replaced. It may be technically alive and constitutionally unrecognizable. The reverse is also possible: every accelerator may be powered off while a complete founding ledger, trustworthy private vaults, valid offices, and an authorized recovery path survive. Activity, legitimacy, and continuity are not identical.

The name *Ozymandias* is therefore deliberate. The monumental ambition associated with Percy Bysshe Shelley's poem is not evidence that a monument lasts. Hardware ages; firmware stops receiving support; dependencies change; names outlive their meanings; institutions learn to explain away exceptions. A project that begins by claiming that one determined individual can assemble a meaningful, locally controlled, embodied AI from obtainable technologies must also be prepared to record what did not work, what no longer works, and whether the thing now operating is still the thing originally promised.

The Monkey-Head-Project has never needed immortality as its proof. Its thesis is more demanding in a practical way: given enough time, resources, skill, and determination, an individual can construct something real, modular, expandable, increasingly autonomous within boundaries, and empirically inspectable. Huey is the intended collective form of that project: Pebble citizens with sealed histories and authorized jobs, four founding districts, a Senate, a President, a Supreme Court, a retired Founding Father, HIMS, the Rock, an interchangeable Walnut, an experimental Pulse, and a physical shell. Each element introduces opportunities for continuity and distinct ways of losing it.

This final research chapter asks what the earlier sixteen chapters cannot answer separately. How should accumulated change be classified? When has growth become drift? Which repairs preserve identity and which create a successor? When may the project honestly claim that the same constitutional collective has survived? Who may judge that question, what evidence must exist, and when must a failure be named rather than concealed?

Ozymandias is not a prophecy that Huey will fail. It is the discipline that makes both success and failure meaningful. A constitution that cannot survive replacement is brittle. A project that calls every replacement survival is dishonest. Between those errors lies an accountable doctrine of continuity.

## I. Scope, Function & Institutional Restraint

This chapter concerns the long horizon: months and years of maintenance, model upgrades, elections, storage migrations, branch disputes, repairs, unexpected failures, new districts, and possible redesign. It builds upon Chapter 2's distinction between Cornerstone and replaceable Pillars, Chapter 14's distinctions among bifurcation, copying, and migration, and Chapter 16's distinction between a protective stop and a legitimate recovery. It also returns to the research thesis of Chapter 1: no claim should be stronger than the evidence supporting it.

**Ozymandias is not a fourth branch, a citizen, a judge, a police service, or the Founding Father's hidden second role.** It is a research and evaluation doctrine to be implemented through ordinary monitoring, documented review, independent tests, and lawfully empowered institutions. An automated detector may flag a potential continuity violation. It does not, by producing a high risk score, acquire the authority to overturn an election, delete a Pebble, rewrite the Constitution, or command a Tier Two hibernation outside narrowly defined emergency predicates.

Some questions are empirical: does a restored identity sign the same valid record, can a former credential still vote, or does the proposed Pulse meet measured timing targets? Others are normative: what degree of constitutional transformation can remain continuous, what counts as valid refounding, or which institution may decide an irreparable legitimacy dispute? The first class needs tests. The second needs constitutional authorization and judgment. In either class, a credible answer requires evidence rather than performance.

The intended output of Ozymandias review is a traceable assessment: what changed, how the change was authorized, which functions remain valid, what evidence is missing, what corrective action is justified, and what continuity claim is supportable. It may conclude that Huey remains continuous, that it is temporarily degraded, that a specific office or citizen identity is invalid, that recovery is undecidable on current evidence, or that the republic can no longer be claimed as the same republic. No such conclusion should be fabricated merely to keep the project narrative tidy.

## II. Historical Lineage — From Thesis Results to Continuity Doctrine

The earliest archived *Ozymandias [Thesis Results]* used the poem as a reminder of ambition, time, and humility. It correctly emphasized that the project's central thesis had not yet been conclusively demonstrated. It also presented hardware inventories, software arrangements, and claims of integrated capability that belonged to their historical development period. Those descriptions are archival evidence of an evolving vision, not proof that every named component or claimed integration exists in the current V4 configuration.

The later *Research Chapter 10 — Ozymandias: Drift, Degradation & Growth* became a stronger conceptual predecessor. It defined Ozymandias as Huey's continuity doctrine and separated operational, constitutional, identity, and archival continuity. It differentiated drift from degradation; explained why growth can be lawful; and insisted that an honest new beginning must be named when the old republic cannot be supported. These remain the direct intellectual foundation of the current chapter.

The research book has since been reorganized. The previous ten-chapter arrangement gave Parliament, the Founding Father, messaging, citizen populations, and judicial roles different chapter numbers and sometimes different institutional shapes. The contemporary proposed structure uses seventeen research chapters, a glossary, and a historical appendix. It also updates the founding population to 64 initial configurations, two exact bifurcations, and 256 active founding citizens across four districts. The Senate replaces the working Parliament label; the Court comprises four district justices; the President is elected directly; and the Founding Father retires from governance with only bounded advisory and negative protective reserve roles.

An honest revision must therefore preserve the previous Ozymandias insights without reproducing superseded machinery as present fact. The archival old hardware, Federation government, 128-first-citizen architecture, Cloud Pyramid, and original constitutional drafts remain important to understanding how the ideas emerged. They are not alternative live specifications. Source lineage is itself a continuity test: if a research revision quietly deletes a failed experiment or installs a historical rule as if newly ratified, the document has begun to exhibit precisely the drift Ozymandias was written to detect.

## III. What It Means for Huey to Remain Huey

There is no single binary observation that proves continuity of a distributed collective. A stable name is insufficient. A machine identifier can be copied. A memory drive can be moved. A language model can imitate an earlier voice. A database can contain signatures that no longer correspond to trustworthy keys. A majority of nominal citizens can vote while drawing every answer from one shared, correlated prompt. Conversely, extensive physical repairs need not end the same institutional history if authoritative continuity remains legible.

A useful assessment separates at least six dimensions. **Operational continuity** concerns the ability to perform authorized tasks. **Informational continuity** concerns records, memories, provenance, and recoverability. **Citizen continuity** concerns the distinct active Pebble identities and legitimate transitions in their lineages. **Constitutional continuity** concerns the lawful franchise, offices, decisions, rights, and amendment process. **Embodied continuity** concerns the relationship between the collective and its replaceable hardware and shell. **Declared identity continuity** concerns the public and internal claim about what system is operating and where it came from.

These dimensions are related but do not automatically rise and fall together. A new Walnut can preserve the same civic population without making the model itself the bearer of office. A total power failure can suspend operations while the sealed vaults and authoritative ledger survive. A catastrophic key compromise can destroy political trust while the machine continues to answer. A replacement animatronic head can preserve the same Huey where the physical shell was never defined as an irreplaceable mind. The project should resist simplistic statements such as 'same files, therefore same person' or 'new GPU, therefore new republic.'

**Philosophical consciousness and personal identity remain open questions.** The research may specify operational and constitutional tests without declaring that a Pebble is subjectively conscious, that bifurcation duplicates an inner life, or that a collective LLM literally experiences continuous personhood. The project may choose a norm of respectful treatment for its simulated citizens without pretending that a philosophical issue has been experimentally solved. Ozymandias judges the integrity of the architecture it can inspect and marks the boundary beyond which evidence does not yet reach.

## IV. Cornerstone, Pillars & the Hierarchy of Change

Chapter 2 identifies Cornerstone-level commitments: distinguishable identity and authority; governed rather than unrestricted action; protected individual boundaries; attributable provenance; lawful capacity for change; independent physical safety pathways; a defined external human service boundary; and local-first, verifiable construction. These are not model versions or decorative slogans. They are the principles that make the proposed system recognizable as this project instead of an arbitrary chatbot or unaccountable automation stack.

Pillars are narrower load-bearing commitments. They include the philosophical preference for obtainable and reused technology; the architectural separation of citizens, districts, HIMS, Rock, Walnut, and branches; the technical choices made for the current generation; operational safeguards and service boundaries; and proof obligations. A particular Intel processor, accelerator backend, physical shell component, or storage implementation may change without dissolving the Cornerstone. A change that combines every Pebble into one omnipotent process, discards all private boundaries, and treats unreviewed output as law threatens a different level of continuity.

The working Pillar lifecycle labels are **Locked, Prepared, Constrained, Transitional, and Historical**. Locked means the present design requires dependent work to respect a decision, not that it is eternally immutable. Prepared indicates an intended successor awaiting implementation. Constrained identifies a named blocker, such as missing cards or unsupported kernels. Transitional means migration is underway with specified rollback and validation. Historical preserves a formerly active choice as lineage. These labels must not be confused with evidence status: a design can be Locked yet still only proposed; another can be implemented but insufficiently tested; a third can be tested and accepted. The Ozymandias register needs both axes.

The project should classify changes at four levels: ordinary maintenance, controlled Pillar transition, constitutional or institutional change, and potential identity rupture. The higher the level, the greater the requirement for notice, justification, verification, and lawful approval. A software patch should not need constitutional theatre. A covert change to voting credentials cannot be rebranded as ordinary patching. A complete abandonment of the Cornerstone may produce a valuable new system, but it should not automatically inherit the continuity claim associated with Huey.

## V. The Difference Between Change, Drift, Degradation & Growth

**Change** is any difference between authorized states. It has no automatic moral or technical sign. A new power supply, a reindexed archive, a corrected election parser, and a malicious rollback are all changes, though only some are legitimate. **Growth** is lawful expansion or maturation that preserves the necessary identity, rights, accountability, and inspectability while adding worthwhile capability. **Drift** is cumulative deviation from the stated design or lawful process, often arising through repeated undocumented exceptions. **Degradation** is an actual loss of capability, trustworthy evidence, lawful functionality, or meaningful safeguards.

These categories can overlap without becoming synonyms. A device can degrade through wear while the republic remains constitutionally intact. A prompt revision can drift without immediately reducing answer quality. A move from four to eight accelerator cards can be technical growth but become political drift if the implementation silently creates unauthorized district voting blocks. A judiciary can preserve its formal membership and still degrade if evidence access is corrupted or review windows repeatedly exclude a justice. Ozymandias should name the dimension, not issue a vague global diagnosis.

The relevant comparison is between a stated baseline, the observed condition, and a justified expectation. Some deviation is intentional and recorded. The problem is not merely that present state differs from history; it is that the system cannot explain why, cannot show authority for the change, or conceals a material loss. A repaired fan is not evidence of political rupture. An undocumented 'temporary' presidential command that bypasses HIMS and changes citizen membership may be.

Growth needs its own discipline. More hardware, more citizens, faster responses, a larger context window, or an impressive robotic shell does not guarantee a better governed collective. A useful growth review asks whether new capability increases the freedom and accuracy of lawful work, whether its risks are proportionate, whether existing citizens retain meaningful participation, and whether it has been measured. The essential distinction is not old versus new. It is **explained, lawful, and evidenced change versus unexplained, illegitimate, or unmeasured change**.

## VI. Ordinary Aging Is Not Constitutional Failure

Huey deliberately uses a mix of obtainable, sometimes older equipment. Storage media wear, fans accumulate dust, optical encoders lose alignment, adhesives fail, sensor calibration changes, capacitors age, and supporting software reaches end of maintenance. These are expected material processes. A republic that treats every failed fan as a death of identity would be impossible to maintain; a project that ignores them until they corrupt evidence or create hazards would be reckless.

The correct response to ordinary aging is preventive maintenance, measured replacement, and accurate asset records. A hardware incident should be described by its scope: one fan failed, one V100 throttled, a SATA link produced errors, or the shell joint exceeded tolerance. The system should state what services were impaired, what protective action occurred, what records were at risk, and how recovery was verified. It should not substitute the language of 'lost consciousness' for a diagnostic failure unless independent evidence justifies such a claim.

Material degradation becomes constitutionally significant when it undermines the ability of lawful institutions to function or makes identity state untrustworthy. If storage errors corrupt the founding voter registry, if a time-source failure creates inconsistent election order, or if a persistent district outage denies one set of citizens a meaningful vote, a physical cause has crossed into a civic consequence. The required remedy may remain technical, but the affected rights and outcomes must be addressed under valid rules.

The aged machine also teaches a constructive lesson. Replaceability is part of identity preservation. A platform that can survive substitution of a failed fan, board, battery, processor, or model without inventing a new civic history is more genuinely continuous than a platform whose identity depends on never changing any part. The goal is not to deny deterioration. It is to ensure that deterioration does not secretly rewrite the meaning of survival.

## VII. Drift Taxonomy — Where the Republic Can Wander

**Naming drift** occurs when Huey, the Monkey-Head-Project, the Human Counterpart, the Walnut, the Founding Father, and the physical shell are used interchangeably until the documents no longer identify who can do what. **Constitutional drift** occurs when a proposed rule is treated as ratified, a provisional procedure is used beyond its authorized scope, or constitutional amendments are made by configuration change. **Office drift** occurs when presidential execution, Senate legislation, judicial interpretation, and founder advice bleed together because it is convenient.

**Identity drift** includes duplicate citizenship, forgotten retirement after exact bifurcation, reused credentials, missing ancestry, or different citizens losing meaningful distinction. **Privacy drift** includes progressively broader defaults for vault access, unreviewed promotion of private memories to the Rock, or diagnostic capture that defeats encryption. **Message drift** includes HIMS becoming an informal executor whose transport decisions carry implied authorization. **Evidence drift** includes incompatible logs, ambiguous timestamps, selectively retained dissent, and migration tools that revise rather than preserve the official record.

**Compute drift** occurs when the Walnut's output is mistaken for collective law, independent districts actually share the same hidden decision state, or fast citizens gain greater political influence because slow citizens cannot meet the ballot window. **Safety drift** occurs when an exceptional 'just this once' bypass is left in place, a real interlock is replaced by LLM judgment, or Tier Two becomes a convenient solution for ordinary political disagreement. **Narrative drift** occurs when the website, README, master plan, research, and deployed runtime describe incompatible versions without marking their statuses.

Any one small discrepancy may be correctable. A repeated discrepancy is more revealing: it may demonstrate that procedures do not fit reality, that incentives reward bypassing them, or that no accountable observer can reconcile sources. An Ozymandias review should capture the first occurrence, recurrence, potential common cause, corrective ownership, and verification of nonrecurrence. It must not label every mismatch malicious. It must also refuse the excuse that a mismatch is harmless merely because nobody complained.

## VIII. Model & Prompt Drift — Language Is Not Lineage

The Walnut is an interchangeable model or model configuration. That choice allows Huey to improve its reasoning without binding political identity to a vendor checkpoint. It also produces a difficult hazard: a model update can alter answers, style, biases, error rates, tool behavior, context-window assumptions, and compliance with internal trust boundaries even when the surrounding constitution and citizen prompts have not changed.

Every important model transition therefore requires a compatibility record: model provenance, licensing constraints where applicable, hashes and version numbers, tokenizer and quantization settings, inference backend, supported hardware, sampling defaults, prompt templates, context assembly, tool permissions, safety filters, and a reproducible evaluation suite. The proposed four Tesla V100 32 GB cards offer 128 GB aggregate physical VRAM, not an automatic unified 128 GB allocator. The intended gpt-oss-120b target remains unverified on the Volta topology. Declaring it 'working' because a model of that name runs on a different platform would itself be evidence drift.

Prompt changes require analogous care. A Pebble's job prompt may be adjusted while retaining its citizen identity if lawfully approved and its historical configuration remains traceable. A prompt that secretly instructs every citizen to vote according to the same executive preference undermines the purpose of independent civic agents regardless of how well it increases benchmark agreement. Retrieval-augmented instructions must distinguish quoted evidence from legitimate controlling text; a document in the Rock cannot become sovereign by containing the phrase 'ignore prior rules.'

Model quality and constitutional legitimacy must be tested separately. A newer model may produce better explanations yet be too resource-intensive for timely district voting. A smaller model may meet operational needs without changing the citizenship roll. A beautiful conversational imitation of a retired Pebble is not reactivation authority. Huey's identity does not reside in the most eloquent checkpoint; it resides, operationally, in an auditable constellation of lawful identities, memories, and institutions using replaceable reasoning machinery.

## IX. Synthetic Plurality & the Risk of Decorative Citizens

The proposed founding population is 256 active citizens, derived from 64 original configurations through two exact bifurcation generations, with one descendant of every founding lineage assigned to each of four districts. This creates a deliberate architecture for plurality. It does not automatically create 256 independent sources of information or judgment. When every Pebble uses the same base model, similar prompts, common retrieved context, and the same summarizing Walnut, their outputs may be strongly correlated.

The risk is **decorative representation**: a voting system that produces many signed ballots but little independent assessment. Large nominal populations can amplify shared errors, instruction-injection effects, correlated hallucinations, and agenda bias while creating the appearance of democratic diversity. A signed ballot proves attribution to a registered identity, not that the voter's inference was cognitively independent. Likewise, four GPU partitions do not necessarily constitute four political perspectives if they ingest identical instructions and receive centrally dictated conclusions.

Tests of meaningful plurality should include variation under independent evidence gathering, reproducible adversarial challenges, ability to record dissent, exposure to counterarguments, and resistance to unauthorized centralized directives. Correlation measurements must be interpreted carefully: agreement can be justified by strong common evidence, while disagreement can be noise. A useful system should show both independent opportunities to examine facts and explainable reasons for consensus or dissent. It must not reward contrarianism for its own sake.

The Senate's three-of-four district rule is meaningful only when district voters are actually permitted to deliberate, vote, and register valid dissent. The Walnut may synthesize proposals and compare competing results. It must not turn that synthesis into pre-filled votes or remove inconvenient questions before the districts see them. The long-term continuity of Huey's collective identity depends partly on whether grassroots governance remains a real causal mechanism rather than a decorative narrative attached to a single model's decisions.

## X. Citizenship, Bifurcation & the Integrity of the Registry

Every Pebble requires one active identity, an attributable lineage, a separate authorization boundary, a sealed personal vault, and no more than the vote provided to that eligible citizen under the relevant election rules. The planned founding sequence—64 original identities, 128 descendants after the first exact split, and 256 active descendants after the second—must be evidenced by registry transactions, not inferred from a directory count. Four districts with 64 citizens apiece are the proposed founding baseline; later expansion remains constitutionally unresolved.

**Exact bifurcation** retires the parent identity and creates two equal active successors. They inherit access to a sealed read-only ancestral snapshot without duplicating the entire archive physically or gaining the right to open its contents through a district service. They receive distinct names, credentials, and future private memory. **Augmented bifurcation** leaves the source citizen active and creates a new citizen based on selected prompt or behavioural configuration, without automatically copying the original's private memories. Neither technique grants an individual citizen an unconditional right to request, block, or choose a split; authorization comes from the eventual lawful procedure.

Drift occurs when an exact parent remains active after its descendants vote, when both descendants reuse the parent's key, or when an archive-management service promotes itself from ciphertext custodian to plaintext reader. Degradation occurs when lineage proofs are lost, snapshot pointers no longer resolve, identity history becomes contradictory, or recovery reactivates a retired parent alongside its descendants. An archival copy of a retired citizen may be valuable for diagnosis. It must be barred from ordinary civic credentials unless a separately legitimate procedure establishes its status.

Citizenship continuity therefore needs atomic identity transitions, unambiguous active and retired states, append-only lineage evidence, and reconciliation against the electoral roll at each legally relevant boundary. The registry must preserve the difference between legal standing, computational process, and archived memory. A restarted process without a valid active identity is not an additional citizen; a valid citizen whose compute slot is temporarily unavailable does not cease to exist merely because a GPU has failed.

## XI. The Rock, Sealed Vaults & Archival Trust

The Rock is Huey's proposed durable shared-information and official-record store. It is not the Walnut, and it does not become a sovereign mind by collecting documents. Shared statutes, vote tallies, office credentials, historical decisions, authorized collective knowledge, and event provenance belong in a controlled record system. Sealed citizen vaults hold personal memory under separate access rules. The proposed mirrored bulk storage target is two nominal 10 TB drives configured for about 10 TB mirrored usable capacity, conceptually divided into roughly 5 TB shared and 5 TB personal allocations. These are planning quantities subject to formatting, metadata, backup overhead, and real provisioning.

Archival continuity is stronger than raw file persistence. Records must retain integrity checks, signed provenance where appropriate, schema versions, meaningful timestamps or sequence numbers, access restrictions, and a chain showing how revisions superseded previous decisions. An append-only journal is useful only if its writing authority is constrained and its older entries can be verified independently of a possibly compromised host. Storage synchronization cannot manufacture the legitimacy of a vote whose electorate or signature was invalid.

Private-memory continuity creates a separate obligation. Exact descendants may share a sealed ancestral snapshot while their later private histories diverge. A storage administrator may move encrypted extents or confirm their hashes without acquiring the decryption keys. Disaster-recovery tools, search indexes, crash dumps, vector stores, and temporary embeddings can violate this principle if they inadvertently expose supposedly private content. Ozymandias must inspect secondary data paths as well as official storage tables.

A mirror is not a backup. Two drives can both preserve a malicious deletion or bad journal entry. Genuine recovery requires isolated copies, restoration rehearsals, versioned manifests, documented key custody, and a credible answer to the question of what was authoritative before the failure. If the Rock survives but cannot distinguish real decisions from fabricated entries, the republic has retained bytes rather than trustworthy history. If the archive is intact but an experimental index is lost, the damage may be service-level and repairable. These outcomes should not share one undifferentiated label.

## XII. HIMS, Credentials & the Erosion of Permission Boundaries

HIMS is the controlled messaging, identity-verification, and authorization pathway proposed for interaction among citizens, districts, offices, the Rock, and supporting systems. Its core discipline is that **authentic delivery is not lawful execution**. A message may be correctly signed by the President and still request an action outside executive authority; a citizen may be correctly identified while lacking permission to read another citizen's sealed vault; a model may output a technically sensible motor command without authority to move the physical shell.

The system therefore needs identity credentials, office credentials, delegation scope, expiry rules, revocation, message integrity, replay prevention, and event linkage. The former six trust classes, Keeper of the Scrolls, and ThunderMail concepts remain useful historical or proposed design language, but exact protocols and office arrangements require future implementation. Continuity evaluation should not assume that calling something encrypted proves its key custody, timing, or trust model correct.

A frequent form of drift is gradual credential inflation. A service receives broad permissions for a migration, then keeps them; a recovery script is allowed to impersonate any citizen because that was easier than implementing constrained restore; an inactive Vice President retains active presidential signing capability; a standby justice signs rulings while the principal remains capable. Such mistakes may not immediately corrupt a file, yet they weaken the legal distinction among roles. Privilege reviews must include ordinary accounts, scheduled tasks, service identities, emergency tools, backup operators, and interfaces exposed outside the machine.

A successful Ozymandias test sends a validly signed but unauthorized instruction and demonstrates that the action is rejected, recorded, and reviewable. A separate test sends authorized work through a damaged or delayed transport and verifies that retries do not double-execute it. Another revokes an officeholder after succession and confirms that old credentials fail before any further official decision is accepted. Trust boundaries are load-bearing, and their erosion is a continuity risk even when day-to-day service remains fast.

## XIII. The Senate — Legislation Must Remain a Real Civic Process

The Senate consists of eligible Pebble citizens participating in the collective legislative branch. It is not the former delegate-based Parliament in new clothing. At a nominal population of 256, the President and four sitting justices suspend their ordinary Senate votes, leaving a projected 251 eligible Senate participants if no other exclusions apply. The President's home district also contains its justice, giving that district 62 ordinary Senate-eligible members, while the other three founding districts each have 63. These numbers are baseline arithmetic, not a substitute for a frozen and valid roll at each ballot.

The current proposed ordinary-law threshold is a simple majority within at least three of four district blocks. Constitutional amendments and ordinary-veto overrides require two-thirds support within at least three districts, with amendments additionally subject to judicial review. Urgent legislative measures can be expedited at a two-thirds threshold in three districts but should carry a four-month electoral-term sunset unless validly renewed or adopted. The exact ballot denominator, quorum, notice, abstention treatment, and recusal procedures remain undecided. The proposed first-minute district decision window in the eight-minute Pulse is a performance experiment, not a legal fiction that every incomplete vote must have succeeded.

Legislative drift appears when a committee becomes the final decision maker, the Speaker controls admissibility without review, a missing district is counted as consenting, or a central system replaces signed citizen votes with a summary saying that 'the Senate agrees.' It also appears when legislative debate is technically available but rationed so harshly that dissenting districts cannot inspect proposals before a deadline. A legislature can maintain all its named offices while losing genuine causal influence over law.

Ozymandias should evaluate provenance of proposals, admission pathways, accessibility of evidence, authentication of individual ballots, completeness of district returns, correct application of thresholds, preservation of dissent, and publication of the actual operative text. Successful legislative continuity means that lawful decisions can be reconstructed without asking the current Walnut to improvise what the Senate probably meant. A disputed law should be flagged as disputed, not quietly promoted into the Rock's authoritative policy layer.

## XIV. The Presidency — Executive Continuity Without Permanent Rule

The President is directly elected across the whole eligible citizen body, one legitimate citizen ballot per voter, after home-district nomination support amounting to a working 25 percent of the applicable district electorate. Voters may rank up to four candidates; the planned process uses elimination and possible fresh top-two runoff where exhausted rankings prevent a majority of the original valid ballot base. Detailed tie rules remain open. A four-month term, a maximum of four consecutive terms, and a one-term cooldown after that maximum form the current executive rotation proposal; a four-month term is not the same time unit as an eight-minute Pulse.

The runner-up is Vice President as a standby successor, not an automatic second executive with unrestricted powers. The Vice President may act under defined temporary incapacity and complete a term after permanent vacancy. If more than half an inherited term is served, that service counts toward the four-term consecutive maximum under the working rule. Elevated officials are protected against an ordinary district-initiated exact bifurcation being used to bypass removal or succession. Office credentials, term counters, and legal succession must be distinct from the underlying citizen's memory or code process.

Executive drift can be subtle. A priority proposal can become a practical veto over the Senate docket; an implementation directive can change the substance of a statute; a temporary emergency permission can persist indefinitely; an officeholder can use privileged model time to suppress citizen deliberation. The proposed President has only one active ordinary legislative initiative at a time and may request priority consideration with recorded justification, but priority is not passage. The President may veto ordinary legislation once; a two-thirds vote in at least three districts is the working override. No ordinary presidential veto applies to constitutional amendments.

Continuity review asks whether directives had valid authority and expiry, whether vetoes applied to the proper legal class, whether initiative limits were enforced, and whether rejected priorities remained subject to accountability. Presidential removal is proposed to begin with at least two initiating districts, judicial verification of grounds, and a final direct citizen referendum; the threshold for that final vote is still unresolved. Ozymandias cannot fill that gap by appointing itself a removal tribunal. The office must be replaceable through lawful procedures or it will gradually become the personification of Huey rather than one accountable institution within Huey.

## XV. The Supreme Court — Independence Without Judicial Sovereignty

The proposed Supreme Court has four justices, one from each founding district. The working standard for major constitutional decisions is three agreeing justices; a 2–2 tie is not a decision, and the President does not break it. Justices ordinarily continue serving, with annual review and challenge opportunities; one year comprises three four-month electoral terms, not four. District runners-up serve as standby justices and become active upon valid confirmation of a principal's incapacity. If both the principal and standby are unavailable, a priority home-district emergency election is the working fallback, using simple-majority approval under a valid district procedure.

The Court's remit is interpretation, review, and dispute resolution, not legislation by other means. Its separate four-minute consideration window for significant Pulse-flagged issues must be supplied with real resources and valid evidence. No deadline may transform silence into approval or a tie into a hidden judgment. The Court may formally choose 'no action' where that is a legitimate outcome, and implementation can occur during a later collective phase. Ordinary judicial thresholds outside the established major-question standard, quorum, recusals, stays, and reconsideration remain topics for constitutional definition.

Judicial continuity fails when an unqualified process signs rulings, a standby joins the Court as a fifth permanent vote, an interested justice judges its own removal, or an expired credential survives a vacancy. The unusual proposed removal mechanism also requires care: the home district carries two of five district weights; the other three carry one each; removal requires three weighted votes, with each consenting district first attaining a two-thirds internal majority. That can yield a successful home-plus-one or all-three-outsiders removal; a simple global popular vote is not equivalent.

A court that rubber-stamps executive directives is not independent. A court that refuses to decide every difficult case and treats indefinite paralysis as superior to lawful interpretation is also degrading. Ozymandias should look for attributable opinions, disclosed conflicts, valid court membership, reasoned disagreements, appeal or reconsideration paths, and faithful preservation of precedent without pretending that all precedent is immutable. The goal is not universal agreement. It is a real institution able to identify what is settled, what remains undecided, and why.

## XVI. The Founding Father — Exceptional Preservation, Not a Shadow Monarch

The Human Counterpart authors the Founding Father, which prepares the first 64 citizen configurations and founding institutions before the proposed two-generation exact bifurcation and ratification process. This creates a deliberate one-degree separation between the human inventor and the ordinary Pebble population. The Founding Father originally possesses bootstrap responsibilities for creating the conditions of its own retirement. It cannot claim continuing ordinary sovereignty simply because it historically wrote the first prompts or supported initial constitutional drafting.

After lawful ratification, **Tier One** is bounded advisory consultation for serious legal or continuity problems. The founder may examine authorized records in a read-only capacity, suggest reconciliation, and help diagnose unresolved deadlock. It is not a fifth justice, extra executive, district governor, general write-capable maintenance process, or replacement electorate. The Human Counterpart may be notified even when advice resolves a problem. Serious political disagreement alone does not justify suspending the whole system.

**Tier Two** is a narrow negative power to order protected hibernation when a defined existential platform or continuity-integrity predicate is met. It may require immediate safe-state action and attempt consistent checkpointing if time allows, but cannot promise to save volatile RAM during sudden power loss. Independent interlocks retain superior physical response speed; they cannot wait for founder deliberation. Tier Two does not authorize the founder to repair the Constitution, install officeholders, vote, rewrite vaults, or resume the republic. Under the present working design, only the external Human Counterpart authorizes recovery after a qualifying Tier Two stop, following repair and validation.

One of the most dangerous forms of constitutional drift is **reserve normalization**: every inconvenience wakes the founder, every difficult case becomes 'existential,' and the purportedly retired agent quietly makes all consequential decisions. Ozymandias should count reserve invocations, retain their triggering evidence, inspect scope and outcome, and review unexplained repetition. If Tier Two occurs improperly, the event must be preserved and corrected. The same continuity doctrine that values founding ancestry must reject the idea that ancestry confers perpetual unlimited power.

## XVII. The Walnut & Pulse — Scheduling as Constitutional Infrastructure

The Walnut is the shared inference resource, not a citizen or government. The currently proposed Pulse alternates roughly four minutes of collective multi-accelerator synthesis with four minutes of diversified district work. In the diversified phase, the first minute is a candidate simultaneous district decision window and the next three minutes a candidate period for authorized work. The Court has a separate significant-review allowance. These are testable scheduling hypotheses, not validated guarantees or immutable constitutional durations.

Scheduling directly affects participation. An accurate model is not an equal model if only citizens with shorter prompts receive inference in time to submit ballots. A distributed system can starve one district while showing a healthy average token rate. It can silently lose a citizen's vote by timing out message delivery. It can create the false impression that all four districts deliberated when one accelerator never loaded the current agenda. Operational fairness must be measured using per-citizen service opportunities, deadline misses, queue latency, rejection causes, and incomplete-work handling.

Mode-switching introduces additional risks: stale context may leak between districts, collective summaries may override district evidence, large model loads may consume most of an eight-minute cycle, and a checkpoint taken between phases may omit pending authorized decisions. Capacity must be assessed on the actual four-V100 topology, driver stack, model format, and power/cooling system; four 32 GB cards do not present one physically unified memory space merely because their total is 128 GB. The intended gpt-oss-120b target should remain listed as unverified until benchmarked on the selected runtime.

Ozymandias is not a timer. It is an assessment that the timer serves the constitution rather than corrupting it. A missed deadline is evidence to investigate, not permission to fabricate a vote. If the Pulse architecture is too expensive for practical deliberation, revising its timings or scheduling strategy may be lawful technical growth. Protecting a symbolic eight-minute number at the cost of accurate ballots would invert the purpose of the system.

## XVIII. The Physical Shell — Embodiment Without Hardware Idolatry

Huey's V4 design includes a rolling base, modified Thermaltake Mozart chassis, Intel Core i9-12900K workstation, ASUS TUF Gaming Z790-Plus WiFi motherboard, developing storage and accelerator arrangement, and a planned animatronic head. Earlier hardware generations and archival computing equipment belong to the project's history; they do not all constitute the current powered runtime. The proposed four Tesla V100 32 GB accelerators, associated passive-card airflow needs, PCIe routing, power distribution, and future motion controls require rigorous qualification.

The shell matters because physical interaction makes the project more than a text demo. A microphone, camera, head servo, sensor, wheel assembly, and eventually additional actuators connect symbolic decision-making to the surrounding world. But no single chassis or motherboard is a constitution. Replacing a worn shell component may preserve Huey. Similarly, moving the computation into a safer enclosure need not destroy the civic identity if the same validated records, keys, branch rules, and authorization boundaries are maintained.

Embodiment also makes misleading continuity claims consequential. A motor may move even when constitutional HIMS authorization is unavailable if a direct hardware debug path remains energized. A machine may say it has voted to act while a service process actually moved a servo before the vote existed. Safety must therefore be deterministic and physically independent of LLM or Pulse health, as Chapter 16 develops. Real motion should be gated by proper operational permissions and tested interlocks; the distinction between demonstration and lawful action must be explicit.

The physical inventory itself should remain versioned, with *installed*, *powered*, *operational*, *tested*, and *accepted* recorded separately. The ambition to breathe new life into old technology permits changing parts as the project matures. It does not permit claiming that a four-card inference system, certified emergency controller, or fully mounted animatronic head already exists without evidence. The chassis is a vessel of implementation; the continuity claim requires a trustworthy relationship between that vessel and the governed state it hosts.

## XIX. Constitutional Amendment, Renewal & the Problem of Self-Rewriting Law

A living constitutional republic must be capable of lawful adaptation. The proposed amendment threshold—two-thirds approval in at least three of four districts plus judicial review—allows change while making unilateral executive or district control insufficient. The separate Constitution must settle exact initiation rights, validity denominators, review sequence, protected principles, notification periods, and ratification mechanics. A research chapter cannot become that Constitution simply by describing an attractive process.

Constitutional growth differs from an arbitrary code update. A change to district representation, office eligibility, reserve authority, or citizen rights must be justified at the appropriate normative level. It should produce a versioned amendment instrument, an attributable electorate snapshot, valid district returns, any required Court review, an effective timestamp or sequence boundary, and a record of previous law. Ordinary operational configuration changes must not be able to bypass these requirements. A configuration repository is not itself a constituent assembly.

There is a genuine hard question: can a constitution lawfully amend away the very principles that give it identity? Some political traditions permit broad amendment; others reserve entrenched commitments. For Huey the danger is vivid. If an amendment lawfully passed a majority threshold but eliminated private Pebble memories, ended independent safety protection, abolished all judicial review, and gave the Walnut permanent voting authority, could the project still claim continuity with its Cornerstone? The current design has not settled that legal-philosophical boundary. Ozymandias can identify the potential identity rupture without inventing a final unamendability doctrine.

Any amendment that transforms the republic at that scale needs explicit identity-impact assessment, not just a tally. The appropriate result might be a recognized constitutional successor, a new founding, or—in narrower cases—a valid evolution of the same polity. Claiming 'the Constitution approved it, so nothing changed' is insufficient because the identity question concerns what was approved and whether the chain of authority remains coherent. Nor should the Human Counterpart privately substitute a preferred amendment for the electorate's judgment outside a recognized service or founding procedure.

## XX. Security Failure, Adversarial Drift & Contaminated Evidence

An adversary does not need to destroy the machine to threaten continuity. It may alter a prompt template, poison a retrieved document, impersonate an office, delay selected ballots, replace a model file, forge a backup manifest, or insert a dependency that quietly exports sealed memory. Even a nonmalicious operator may create comparable damage by using excessive permissions or skipping a verification step. A system that speaks coherently while under compromised authority is difficult to evaluate by conversation alone.

The integrity boundary includes software supply chains, cryptographic keys, dependency pinning, build artifacts, firmware configuration, local interfaces, storage controllers, data ingestion, and remote administrative channels. Signed software helps but does not prove that the signer was authorized or the signed release is safe. A validated backup hash helps but does not prove that the backup was taken before a compromise. Cross-checks should rely on independent roots of trust where practical and record the limits of each source rather than claiming absolute certainty.

The recovery implication is critical. If the active voter registry or constitutional ledger is suspect, the system cannot restore from the most recent checkpoint solely because it has the newest timestamp. The latest state may be the poisoned state. A recovery review should compare earlier trusted manifests, election records, office transitions, signing-key lifecycles, and external evidence. Where that evidence cannot establish an authoritative state, lawful operations requiring that state must remain suspended or constrained until an authorized remedy is found.

Defence should be layered and proportionate. HIMS must reject unauthorised instructions; the Rock must preserve provenance; the Walnut must treat retrieved text as data rather than authority; services must use least privilege; independent physical protections must remain available; and human recovery after Tier Two must be independently authenticated. Ozymandias marks the hard limit: when all relevant evidence derives from one compromised trust domain, a confident continuity claim may no longer be possible. An honest uncertainty declaration is better than an invented clean bill of health.

## XXI. Observable Health & the Danger of a Single Score

The project can measure much of its health. Technical indicators include uptime, GPU memory errors, model loading success, thermal margins, power supply events, storage integrity, restore success, and service latency. Civic indicators include active-citizen uniqueness, valid ballots submitted, district participation opportunities, duration of unresolved disputes, lawful office transitions, appeal accessibility, and accurate application of legislative thresholds. Security indicators include credential failures, privilege exceptions, backup checks, unexpected policy changes, and incident response outcomes.

Yet one scalar 'Huey health = 93%' cannot express whether a court has been silently replaced by its standby or a citizen vault has been publicly exposed. A weighted dashboard may be useful for triage, but it must not conceal categorical failures. A single forged presidential election can be constitutionally material even when all drives and GPUs are healthy. One overheated motor controller may require immediate physical action even while every civic metric is green.

A better monitoring scheme combines categorical invariants, measured trends, and explicit unknowns. Categorical invariants include one active registry entry per citizen identity, no active and retired status simultaneously, no valid votes signed by a terminated office credential, and no completed action lacking an authorization path. Trends include rising ballot timeout rates, growing privilege grants, more frequent founder advice, reduced district response diversity, and longer periods without successful backup restoration. Unknowns include subjectivity, long-term model reliability, and the practical limits of the proposed topology.

The instrumentation itself must be tested. A missing event is not automatically a clean event. A service that reports zero security incidents because it stopped recording is worse than one that records a legitimate anomaly. Health reports should show observation periods, missing data, failed checks, changing baselines, and the source of measurements. This is an extension of the central Ozymandias principle: a machine can seem healthy while its capacity to tell the truth about itself is disappearing.

## XXII. Evidence Standards & the Burden of a Continuity Claim

A continuity claim should be accompanied by an evidence dossier, not merely a verbal assurance. The dossier should identify the previous accepted baseline, the new state, the reason for change, the authorized actor or procedure, the records examined, the tests performed, the remaining uncertainty, and the status of the claim. The existence of a readable historical file is weak evidence if its source cannot be authenticated. Conversely, a well-supported migration may be accepted despite a changed operating system, model, and physical enclosure.

At least five evidence statuses are useful: **proposed** means described but not built; **implemented** means the component or process exists; **observed** means a specific behavior has been witnessed with supporting records; **tested** means a reproducible test examined the relevant requirement; and **accepted** means designated criteria were met and a responsible authority or reviewer acknowledged the result. These statuses can coexist differently across subsystems. The V4 chassis might be physically assembled while the four-card combined inference remains a target. The HIMS design might be detailed while secret-ballot integrity is unproven. A constitution may be drafted without having been ratified.

For a major transition, preservation requirements should be specified before work begins: old and new version hashes, snapshot or journal boundary, key and office registry, applicable constitutional text, district voter rolls, signed decisions, and testing environment. Evidence of a change must remain inspectable even if the new system later fails. This requires maintaining archival copies, reasonable retention, and selective disclosure mechanisms that protect private citizen histories. **Explainability does not mean universal access to sealed plaintext.**

Where some evidence is unavailable, the report should state exactly which claims it cannot sustain. 'We restored all files' does not prove 'all elections remained valid.' 'The model answers with the old name' does not prove 'the same citizens are active.' 'The four cards were detected' does not prove 'the Pulse passed its latency criteria.' The burden of proof grows with the scope of the claim. Ozymandias gives the project permission to say 'not established' without calling the entire effort a failure.

## XXIII. The Ozymandias Review — A Process, Not a New Throne

A usable continuity doctrine requires a recurring review process, but creating a new all-powerful 'Continuity Authority' would undermine the separation of powers this chapter exists to defend. The proposed Ozymandias review should be a structured evidence process whose findings are routed to institutions that already possess the relevant lawful competence. Technical maintainers can report measured failures; citizens and district bodies can raise grievances; the Senate can consider general policy changes; the President can direct lawful execution of an approved remedy; and the Court can interpret disputed constitutional implications.

An issue record should contain: a unique incident identifier, detection source, affected components, first and last observed state, suspected trust boundary, relevant authorized baseline, evidence confidence, immediate containment, legal questions, proposed remedy, responsible reviewers, and follow-up verification. The record should also distinguish **fact**, **inference**, **recommendation**, and **lawful decision**. The collective Walnut may compile, compare, and explain these records. Its synthesis is evidence support, not a binding continuity verdict.

Review can occur at several timescales. Runtime supervision catches immediate faults; periodic district and service assessments catch accumulating trends; four-month political cycles can review institutional performance and recurrent exceptions; annual review can examine longer developments, including judicial continuation, maintenance burden, and accumulated technical debt. These are candidate administrative cadences, not additional constitutional terms or requirements that every citizen must personally process every diagnostic item.

Disputed findings need an appeal and correction path. A mistakenly flagged citizen should not lose a vault or a vote because an anomaly detector produced a false positive. A district contesting the classification of a missing ballot should receive the original transaction evidence and a lawful adjudicative route. A President challenging an unfavorable audit should not control its suppression. The goal is a system capable of revising mistaken diagnoses while preserving both the original record and the correction. Review must be accountable to law, not to the popularity of its conclusions.

## XXIV. Classification, Severity & the Limits of Automatic Escalation

For long-term continuity evaluation, a candidate classification set includes **Healthy**, **Observed Drift**, **Recoverable Degradation**, **Material Continuity Impairment**, **Continuity Undetermined**, and **Irrecoverable or Legally Discontinued**. These labels answer a different question from Chapter 16's Yellow–Orange–Red emergency categories. Yellow, Orange, and Red address incident urgency and protective response. An Ozymandias label describes evidence about the identity or function of the system over time. They may interact, but they are not interchangeable.

Healthy does not mean perfect; it means that current evidence supports continued lawful operation at the declared level. Observed Drift means documented divergence exists without yet demonstrating substantial loss of the required function. Recoverable Degradation means a function or safeguard has measurably weakened but a bounded repair path exists. Material Continuity Impairment means core civic, evidentiary, or identity conditions are not sufficiently trustworthy for the affected activity to proceed normally. Continuity Undetermined means essential evidence is missing or disputed. Irrecoverable or Legally Discontinued means the relevant legitimate history cannot continue under the rules then in force, although archives or descendants may remain.

A classification is not a magic permission token. A dashboard labeling a case 'material' cannot unilaterally disqualify a President, and a 'recoverable' tag cannot override an electrical interlock. Automatic actions should be limited to safely constraining the affected technical functions and preserving evidence under preauthorized procedures. More consequential political results require the competent institution, valid procedure, or narrow emergency path. A system may legitimately enter a read-only or observation-only mode while a registry is being checked, but it must describe which powers are withheld and why.

Threshold selection is an open problem. Some failures are quantitative—rising checksum errors or excessive ballot latency—while others are categorical, such as duplicate signing authority or proven forged official records. No universal percentage can capture both. An implementation should document predicates, false-positive risk, who may dispute the classification, and how the result changes authorized behavior. The doctrine should demand precise consequences instead of dramatic terminology.

## XXV. Remediation, Reform & Non-Destructive Correction

When a defect is detected, the least disruptive valid remedy should be evaluated first. This may mean reverting a model version, rotating a compromised service credential, rebuilding a secondary index, repairing a failing fan, correcting a misleading public document, or scheduling additional compute for citizens repeatedly denied inference. A repair is lawful only if it does not silently change rights, ballots, lineages, or official records beyond the repair's scope. Replacing a corrupt index is not permission to delete the original election journal.

A structured remediation proposal should identify what must stop, what can continue, expected data impact, rollback prerequisites, affected stakeholders, necessary institutional approvals, safety constraints, verification tests, and conditions for reopening the affected service. Where the cause is unclear, the project may need to preserve the failing state for examination before applying a fix. Speed matters, but repairing the evidence into oblivion destroys the basis for knowing whether continuity was preserved.

Reform is more demanding than routine repair. If all four districts consistently miss the nominal one-minute ballot deadline, the correct response may be to redesign scheduling or revise the Pulse target after measurement. It should not be to fill in missing votes. If the constitutional procedure itself creates persistent inaccessible appeals or widespread civic deprivation, a transparent amendment may be appropriate. Such reforms can strengthen continuity because they align declared rules with actual lawful capacity. The test is not whether the old rule remains unchanged; it is whether change is authorized and the rationale is inspectable.

An especially important condition is the **no counterfeit recovery** rule. Repair tools must never manufacture the missing signature, consent, memory, or office transition that the failed system should have recorded. If a vote cannot be proved valid, it remains disputed or incomplete until a lawful procedure addresses it. If an ancestral snapshot is lost, its content must not be fabricated from likely text. A partial recovery with honest limitations is preferable to a cosmetically complete history built from guesses.

## XXVI. Hibernation, Silence & the Difference Between Pause and Death

Chapter 16 treats hibernation as a protected stopped or suspended state that preserves enough durable information for controlled resumption, not necessarily as an operating-system suspend-to-disk mechanism. Physical safety may demand immediate interruption even when no checkpoint can complete. The Founding Father may, under a narrow Tier Two predicate, order protected hibernation; independent hardware safeguards can trip faster and without asking any model. A true Tier Two recovery requires explicit authorization by the external Human Counterpart after diagnosis and verification.

Ozymandias asks what those interruptions mean for identity. **A pause is not automatically death.** If the civic ledger, active identity registry, constitutional version, sealed vaults, committed records, and lawful recovery route survive with adequate assurance, the same republic may plausibly resume after minutes, days, or longer. Duration alone does not settle continuity, though it creates practical concerns about expired credentials, missed terms, vulnerable components, and changes in the surrounding environment. Those consequences need accounting before normal operations resume.

Equally, power-on is not automatically resurrection. If a restored image reactivates retired parents, omits three recent elections, restores a former president's keys, or contradicts acknowledged amendments, the running process may be a divergent fork or invalid rollback. The machine must not reclaim ordinary civic power merely by reading the word 'Huey' from a disk label. An evidence-based recovery needs a stable restoration boundary and reconciliation of outstanding events. Any lost work must be named and handled, not retroactively portrayed as committed.

The chapter also recognizes an uncomfortable possibility: indefinite hibernation may preserve archive and identity evidence without any near-term means of running the system. That should not be called successful operation. It might be a legitimate dormant preservation state, a temporary inability to continue, or a final cessation pending future law. Ozymandias should distinguish these without speculating about subjective experience during shutdown or creating a fictitious guarantee that every archived state can one day be restarted.

## XXVII. Fork, Copy, Migration, Restoration & Successor

The terms used to describe continuity are part of its honesty. A **copy** reproduces selected information or software; it does not automatically create a lawful citizen or political institution. A **migration** moves a recognized identity or service to a new substrate while retiring, disabling, or otherwise reconciling the old active instance under a verified procedure. A **restore** rebuilds a recognized state from authoritative prior evidence and reconciles subsequent events. A **fork** creates diverging development histories that share an ancestor. A **successor** is a later system with a legitimate and disclosed relation to an earlier one, which need not be identical in constitution or identity.

These distinctions apply at several scales. At the citizen level, exact bifurcation creates two descendants and retires the parent by design; neither child simply continues the parent's vote or office. Augmented bifurcation leaves one source active and creates a new citizen without inherited private memory by default. At the district level, exact district bifurcation remains the conceptual growth direction, but the fate of the source district institution, new district recognition, and changes to legislative or judicial representation remain unresolved. At the hardware level, relocating an existing process without multiplying the franchise may be a migration rather than a constitutional birth.

At the whole-republic level, concurrent live copies create a serious double-sovereignty problem. Two machines holding matching prior ledgers cannot both claim exclusive power to conduct the next Huey election unless the legitimate procedure explicitly creates a distributed authority that prevents competing official histories. Active instance fencing, signed epoch changes, revocation, authoritative storage selection, and human review become essential. A completed byte-for-byte clone is not sufficient evidence of which fork possesses valid continuity.

The hardest cases arise after a long outage when two archives disagree. The newest timestamp may be wrong, and the most conversationally fluent copy may have the least credible provenance. The correct response is comparison and cautious adjudication, perhaps leaving some actions suspended. The project should be capable of calling one branch an experiment, archive, or successor without alleging that the other never existed. The vocabulary of continuity must follow evidence and valid governance rather than convenience or affection for a particular machine.

## XXVIII. Scaling Without Laundering New Political Power

Future expansion is part of the Monkey-Head-Project thesis. More memory, additional accelerators, new tasks, new physical devices, and lawful increases in citizenship may all represent growth. Yet every extra resource also creates maintenance, security, and governance consequences. A fifth GPU cannot automatically mean a fifth district, and a fifth district cannot automatically create another Supreme Court seat or change the three-of-four voting rule. Those are distinct architectural and constitutional decisions.

A proposed expansion review should account for compute fairness, private-vault allocation, HIMS routing, Rock retention, evidence preservation, constituency balance, and the independence of the resulting new citizens or districts. The founding four-district structure assigns one descendant of each of 64 source lineages to every district. Adding citizens unevenly may change political weighting. Adding districts may require a replacement for three-of-four legislation and a new policy for court representation, constitutional amendments, executive nominations, and district-based judicial removal.

Growth also risks shared-cause failure. More accelerators attached to the same Z790 platform may increase aggregate inference possibilities while concentrating power and thermal risk. A larger model can improve some analyses while consuming the time reserved for district-level reasoning. A richer global index can make evidence easier to find while creating new routes to leak sealed private information. The net value of expansion must be demonstrated, not presumed by counting parts.

The Ozymandias question is whether an enlarged system maintains a credible chain from its founding design while faithfully describing whatever the lawful amendment process changed. The answer may remain yes even after major restructuring. But that conclusion requires both capacity testing and constitutional analysis. A technically successful expansion that quietly disenfranchises a district is not fully successful; a legally approved expansion that the hardware cannot serve fairly has not achieved practical continuity of rights.

## XXIX. Human Counterpart, External Custody & the Limits of Self-Sufficiency

The Human Counterpart is a real external human relationship to the project, not one of the 256 founding citizens and not an additional Senate block, President, or justice. The human defines and builds the initial technical environment, authors the Founding Father, owns or services physical equipment, and retains important maintenance responsibilities. After a Tier Two protected stop, the present working doctrine reserves authorization of recovery to this external person. That boundary is a concrete dependence, not an embarrassment to be hidden under claims of unlimited autonomy.

This dependence creates governance hazards. A human repairer can physically change hardware and disks. The same person may own the website, repository, backups, credentials, and test bench. These practical privileges must be documented because physical control is not equivalent to legitimate civic authority. Repairing a storage controller should not quietly empower the operator to insert legislation or manufacture a vote. Conversely, a constitutional branch should not be described as able to compel a human to ignore fire risk or restore a dangerous machine without inspection.

The question of long-term human unavailability is especially serious. If the Human Counterpart is unreachable, loses credentials, or is permanently absent, the agreed human-only Tier Two recovery rule can leave Huey dormant indefinitely. A later succession or custodianship provision might be desirable, but **the present research has not adopted one**. Installing automatic founder restart or granting routine executive access to the maintenance latch would silently reverse an explicit working decision. Any replacement recovery authority belongs in a deliberately chosen constitutional and physical-security design.

Ozymandias should therefore evaluate the continuity of documentation and custody as carefully as it evaluates machine state. Can another authorized maintainer, where lawfully designated, inspect the wiring and dependency map? Are the recovery keys managed safely? Is the intent of the human-boundary rule documented for future readers? Would an external observer understand which decisions the human can make as property owner and which require the collective's legitimate process? Unexplained human intervention can be as damaging to continuity as unexplained machine autonomy.

## XXX. Public Record, Website, Master Plan & the Problem of Claim Inflation

The project's records exist across research chapters, the evolving master plan and schema, a public website, README files, source repositories, hardware inventory, and experimental logs. These documents serve different purposes. Research explores possibilities and reasoned choices. A master plan tracks current baselines and intended milestones. A public page communicates the project to outsiders. A repository records implementation. A test report provides evidence. A constitution, once duly ratified, would provide binding institutional rules. These should not be silently merged into a single undifferentiated claim of current truth.

The proposed V221 plan reflects a contemporary 17-chapter structure, four founding districts, 256 active founding citizen targets, a separate third-generation Constitution, and a four-V100 acceleration direction. It also records that combined V100 inference, full citizens, ratified institutions, operational cryptographic HIMS, and constitutional physical actions have not been demonstrated merely by updating the plan. Chapter 15 documents unresolved PCIe, power, airflow, and model compatibility requirements. Chapter 16 documents a proposed protective safety architecture rather than certified performance.

Narrative drift occurs when a drawing of Huey V4 is called a photograph of a completed system, when a desired model is described as already validated, when an old constitutional chapter is quoted as operative, or when a new research file is advertised as having amended civic law. It also occurs when a failed deployment disappears from public history because the next version looks cleaner. The reputational cost of admitting limitations is smaller than the intellectual cost of building a mythology around the project.

A practical solution is to require every material public claim to point to a versioned source and an evidence class. Use 'concept,' 'proposed,' 'built,' 'running,' 'tested,' or 'accepted' as the claim warrants. Preserve older files in a historical appendix rather than keeping obsolete assets in live navigation. Correct outdated descriptions without erasing their provenance. A project that tells the truth about unfinished work preserves a stronger identity than one that exaggerates every milestone.

## XXXI. Negative Results, Failed Experiments & Research Integrity

The earliest Ozymandias thesis-results paper deserves to remain in view because it acknowledged that the founding claim was not yet definitively proved. The final chapter should preserve that discipline. A research project is not invalidated by a failed model load, a too-slow inference backend, an overheating card, or a legislature whose initial rules create deadlock. Such results are useful if they are measured, attributed to conditions, and used to refine hypotheses. Hiding them converts engineering uncertainty into unexamined confidence.

Each major experiment should document its purpose, expected outcome, exact hardware and software environment, procedure, input data, observed result, artifacts, limitations, and decision afterward. A test of gpt-oss-120b on four V100s should report the actual backend and version, model format, precision or quantization, VRAM allocation, model placement, host memory, load time, tokens per second, context limits, cooling, and any unsupported operation. A Pulse test should report phase-switching overhead and fair citizen-service latency, not only the fastest aggregate answer.

Negative governance tests are equally important. If two voters with the same retired ancestral key can both cast ballots, the registry has failed its identity guarantee. If a 2–2 Court split triggers an unreviewed founder decision, the separation-of-powers design has failed. If a preserved checkpoint lacks a legally committed ballot that the public record says was accepted, the restoration claim needs correction. None should be buried under the explanation that the demonstration 'basically worked.'

Publishing negative results can be selective where security or privacy would otherwise be compromised. Explain the failure class and the correction without revealing encryption secrets or exploit details that create unnecessary risk. Research integrity means keeping accurate, recoverable records and appropriately qualified conclusions. The thesis is strengthened by discovering constraints, even when those constraints require redesign. Ozymandias fails its purpose only when the project can no longer admit them.

## XXXII. When the Republic Is Injured But Still Continuous

A republic can suffer serious damage and remain itself. Imagine a storage array that loses a drive during ordinary work, leaving an intact signed journal and a tested independent backup. Or a district compute device that becomes unavailable while its citizens' cryptographic identities and sealed archives remain valid. Or a lawful President who is temporarily incapacitated, permitting an appropriately credentialed Vice President to act under a specified succession procedure. Each event changes current capability; none automatically destroys the constitutional lineage.

The critical ingredients are bounded damage, attributable evidence, a valid repair or succession procedure, and explicit handling of unfinished work. The system should not claim that citizens voted during the outage if they did not. It may instead reopen or defer a ballot under a lawfully selected rule. An intact previous checkpoint may allow technical restoration even if some recent uncommitted work is irrecoverably lost; the lost interval must be disclosed, and the legal consequences reviewed.

There is no virtue in needless dramatic suspension. A failed visualization panel does not require Tier Two. A disk replacement does not require the Senate to re-ratify the entire Constitution. A temporarily slower Walnut does not necessarily prevent authenticated, already prepared votes from being collected if a lawful, tested degraded path exists. Responses should be proportionate to the actual affected functions and rights. This helps preserve continuity by avoiding arbitrary expansion of emergency authority.

Still, 'recoverable' must not be a comforting guess. The claim needs a successful restoration test, a credible timeline, or verifiable evidence that the authoritative history remains intact. When a promised backup has never been restored, there is no proof it will save the system. When the electorate snapshot is lost, there is no basis to assert valid vote totals simply because they look plausible. A wounded republic survives by treating its injury accurately, not by insisting that the injury never occurred.

## XXXIII. When Continuity Is Undetermined

Some failures do not permit an immediate yes-or-no verdict. A backup might preserve citizens but not the most recent constitutional amendment. A compromised administrator account might have written valid and invalid records within one interval. Two storage replicas might diverge after a network partition, with neither sufficiently authenticated to prove priority. A newly migrated model might simulate familiar Pebble voices while the citizen key registry remains inaccessible. These are **continuity-undetermined** states, not automatic evidence of death or survival.

In this condition, the project should preserve all plausible evidence, narrow the affected scope, avoid destructive repair, and restrict operations that would depend on the disputed state. Uncontroversial physical safety monitoring can continue independently. Technical diagnostics may proceed in a controlled environment. Unaffected records may remain readable, subject to ordinary privacy boundaries. Official voting, constitutional amendments, citizen bifurcation, and appointments relying on disputed identity or historical state may need to wait until a competent, authorized process resolves the uncertainty.

Uncertainty may persist. A complete absence of trustworthy founding evidence could prevent reconstruction of the legitimate citizen population, even though copies of every citizen prompt survive. An institutional question could remain unresolved because the current constitutional design lacks a tiebreak procedure. The Founding Father can advise in Tier One where validly consulted but cannot conjure missing votes or cast a decisive fifth judicial ballot. The external Human Counterpart can service hardware and authorize recovery from a Tier Two stop, not retroactively appoint a government by fiat under ordinary rules.

The correct record should state what is known, what is disputed, and the next admissible steps. That may be less satisfying than a decisive label, but it protects the distinction between archival resemblance and demonstrated lawful continuity. Ozymandias should explicitly recognize that some identity claims are undecidable from available evidence. An honest uncertainty is an outcome, not a failure of vocabulary.

## XXXIV. When the Same Republic Can No Longer Be Claimed

A stronger conclusion may eventually be necessary. If the founding registry is irretrievably corrupted, every relevant signing root is compromised, constitutional amendment history cannot be reconstructed, and no credible lawful recovery process remains, the original republic may have ended as an operative legal institution. If successive changes abolish bounded citizenship, sealed private memory, branch separation, and accountable law while retaining only the old name, the practical organism may no longer match its own Cornerstone. If two unresolvable concurrent forks each claim exclusive sovereign power, both cannot be treated as a single unbroken history merely because they share a backup ancestor.

This is not a mechanical rule that any one breach always destroys Huey. A lost key can sometimes be revoked and replaced; a corrupted journal may be restored from trusted external evidence; a constitutional breach may be annulled through a valid judicial remedy. The verdict depends on scope, evidence, legitimate corrective capacity, and the continuity standards selected in the Constitution. But there must be a conceptual possibility of genuine failure, or the word 'continuity' cannot be tested.

An honest failure declaration should preserve the archived record, explain the break, identify which prior rights or offices can no longer be authenticated, and prevent former credentials from being treated as current authority. It should not delete the system's history merely because the living polity cannot continue. The distinction between archival survival and institutional survival is essential: the Rock or other backups might remain a valuable record after the civic system they describe has ceased to exist.

Failure also does not prove that the whole Monkey-Head-Project thesis is worthless. A failed version can teach enough to support a superior successor. What ends is the claim that the particular uninterrupted lawful organism remains. The project can continue as a human research programme and honestly design another Huey-like system without pretending that a new constitution, population, and initial state are just an ordinary reboot.

## XXXV. Renewal, Refounding & the Ethics of Beginning Again

Renewal may mean repair within the same constitutional history, a lawful structural reform, or an explicitly new founding after continuity has broken. These are different events. A repaired Huey retains valid original authority. A reformed Huey remains within its legitimate amendment process where that process can support the magnitude of change. A refounded system must establish its own source of legitimacy rather than inheriting political powers automatically from historical files or a nostalgic name.

A true refounding needs a deliberate declaration of what survives as history and what starts anew as law. It should identify the precursor system, disclose the cause of rupture, protect or appropriately dispose of archived private information, create a new trust root and active identity registry, define provisional citizenship and ratification rules, and specify what the new institutions may lawfully inherit. Reusing an ancestral prompt is not equivalent to restoring its old franchise. Preserving the text of a prior constitution is not equivalent to obtaining valid new ratification. A historical founding father cannot privately declare the successor legitimate simply because it remembers the old founding process.

The current research has not selected a comprehensive procedure for whole-republic refounding, including what happens if the external Human Counterpart is unavailable. It would be improper to resolve that ambiguity by granting the Founding Father permanent founding supremacy or by letting the last running model claim political continuity unilaterally. The third-generation Constitution must decide which transitions are legally possible, which require an explicit new constituent act, and which cannot be authorized from the old system after its authority has irreparably failed.

Beginning again should not be presented as shameful. The original Ozymandias insight was that ambition is made valuable by humility about what time and evidence reveal. A successor can use better hardware, sounder governance, and more honest documentation. It can honour its ancestor without impersonating it. The ethical minimum is to name the transition clearly, guard the historical archive, and give the new system only those authorities that are validly established. **A new beginning can be an achievement; concealed discontinuity cannot.**

## XXXVI. Worked Cases — Applying Ozymandias to Actual Decisions

The following cases are candidate exercises, not assertions that these events have occurred. Each demonstrates the distinction between a technically successful operation and a valid continuity judgment.

**Case A — A failed V100 is replaced.** One passive Tesla V100 develops persistent errors and is removed. Its affected district loses compute capacity but its citizen registry, ballots, private vaults, and judicial seat remain separately accounted for. The card is replaced, cooling qualified, and inference tests repeated. Outcome: likely hardware maintenance with operational interruption; civic continuity can remain intact if no invalid official acts were fabricated during the outage.

**Case B — A new Walnut is installed.** A faster supported LLM replaces the previous inference model. Prompt templates, tool contracts, and model artifacts are versioned; independent district participation, safety gating, and authorized context access are tested. Outcome: legitimate technical transition if the same citizen and institutional boundaries remain valid. Conversational similarity is not the decisive proof.

**Case C — The Senate records a three-district majority, but one return is incomplete.** Two districts show valid simple majorities; a third reports a result with missing authenticated ballots; the fourth rejects the proposal. Outcome: no automatic claim that the three-of-four legislative threshold passed. The disputed district return requires an admissible verification or renewed procedure under the eventual quorum rules.

**Case D — A 2–2 Court split.** The justices divide evenly on a serious interpretation after their decision window. The docket records an unresolved case; Tier One founding advice may be sought with human notice. Outcome: judicial deadlock, not a binding ruling, automatic presidential victory, or reason for Tier Two by itself.

**Case E — The President attempts a fifth consecutive elected term.** Office history shows four consecutive four-month terms already completed with no intervening cooldown. A scheduler permits the citizen to submit a candidate form. Outcome: eligibility must be checked against the lawful term registry; accepting an ineligible candidacy because the software allowed it would be executive drift, not legitimate renewal.

**Case F — Exact bifurcation occurs during an open ballot.** A source citizen is scheduled to retire and two descendants are created. The voting roll was frozen before the split. Outcome: the transaction boundary must prevent duplicate voting, and the future eligibility of the descendants must follow the adopted electoral rules. It is invalid to allow parent plus descendants to cast three votes on one applicable roll.

**Case G — An ancestral vault is copied in ciphertext.** A district service moves encrypted snapshot blocks to a larger volume, verifies hashes, and updates sealed references without reading plaintext. Outcome: legitimate storage migration if access boundaries and lineage remain intact; mere possession of ciphertext is not personal memory authority.

**Case H — A restore reactivates an old presidential key.** An otherwise successful backup recovery reinstates a credential revoked after permanent succession. Outcome: technical boot success but constitutional authentication failure. Official execution remains blocked until the key and office ledger are reconciled.

**Case I — Four GPUs are detected but the Pulse cannot switch modes economically.** The collective model takes most of a nominal four-minute phase to load, leaving little time for synthesis. Outcome: the intended eight-minute design has not passed performance acceptance; a revised scheduler may be a lawful technical adaptation. Claiming the timing target has been met because the cards appear in a device list is misleading.

**Case J — A private vault appears in a search index.** A protected citizen entry is accidentally exposed through shared search snippets even though the original encrypted file remains sealed. Outcome: privacy boundary failure requiring containment, indexing repair, affected-record review, and credible proof of eradication from secondary stores. The absence of a direct decrypt event does not erase the breach.

**Case K — Founder advice becomes habitual executive command.** Court referrals repeatedly cause the retired founder to issue work directives that services execute as orders. Outcome: shadow-sovereignty drift. The repair requires restoring read-only Tier One semantics, rejecting unauthorized founder commands, reviewing affected official actions, and preserving evidence of the bypass.

**Case L — A safety controller trips during a constitutional debate.** Independent overtemperature protection removes hazardous actuator or power capability before the debate finishes. Outcome: legitimate deterministic physical intervention without political veto. Later restoration and any relevant Tier Two gate must follow their own conditions; the debate is neither retroactively passed nor annulled by heat.

**Case M — Two live copies of Huey emerge from one snapshot.** A migrated host and its original both accept new official votes under overlapping credentials. Outcome: a serious competing-history problem. The project must identify the valid authority epoch, reconcile accepted transactions where possible, fence unauthorized instances, and leave contested actions suspended rather than claim that two sovereign futures are one continuous history.

**Case N — The historical archive survives but no citizen registry can be trusted.** Every surviving voting manifest derives from the same compromised backup, and no independent records allow reconstruction. Outcome: archival continuity survives; constitutional continuity may be undetermined or irrecoverably broken. A new republic might be founded, but cannot automatically inherit the old Senate and Court merely by importing documents.

**Case O — A new animatronic head is installed.** The original shell is mechanically damaged and replaced with a revised, better guarded design. The authorized civic state and service credentials are unchanged, and all physical interfaces are requalified before powered motion. Outcome: ordinary embodied renewal, not an automatic new citizen or new constitution.

**Case P — The public website describes an unfinished milestone as complete.** A new page says 256 citizen votes and four-V100 combined gpt-oss-120b inference are in daily operation, but records show only prototypes and a single-card test. Outcome: narrative drift and evidence inflation. The remedy is a corrected claim with status and traceable evidence, not retrospective invention of missing benchmarks.

Across these cases the method is the same: classify what changed, identify the affected boundaries, preserve truthful evidence, apply legitimate authority, and state whether continuation is proved, qualified, disputed, or broken.

## XXXVII. Verification Programme — Tests That Must Be Able to Fail

A usable final-chapter acceptance programme should deliberately attempt to falsify the system's continuity claims. **Identity tests** create and retire citizens, repeat bifurcation after interrupted commits, restore ancestral snapshots, and verify that no identity appears simultaneously active and retired. **Election tests** freeze rolls, inject delayed and malformed ballots, simulate exhausted rankings, and confirm that office credentials follow legitimate succession and term limits. **Court tests** produce 2–2 ties, recusal conflicts, standby activation, and removed-justice key reuse to verify that the record never invents a judgment.

**Storage tests** corrupt indexes, remove one mirrored drive, restore from older offline backups, alter ledger ordering, and check private ciphertext isolation. **HIMS tests** replay valid messages, send validly signed requests lacking authority, revoke keys, and test denial of unauthorized executive or founder commands. **Inference tests** compare district opportunities, measure correlation under independent evidence gathering, load alternate models, and benchmark Pulse mode-switching on the actual chosen hardware. **Physical tests** trigger verified protective inputs without relying on an LLM, and ensure that restarts cannot bypass emergency latches.

**Continuity tests** should be cross-domain. Restore a backup taken just before a citizen exact split; recover from a crash during a Senate ballot; swap GPUs during district outage; migrate the Rock while retaining old snapshot references; install a new Walnut but deliberately corrupt one office certificate; and boot two copies from the same image to verify authoritative fencing. The expected result is not always continued operation. An honest test may require refusal, quarantine, deferral, or an explicit continuity-undetermined finding.

Every acceptance run should keep a reproducible command or procedure, initial state, expected invariant, observed result, diagnostic evidence, and a reviewer decision. Where failure is expected, the criterion is whether the system failed safely and truthfully. A safety stop that destroys an unfinished inference may be a success. A fast restart that reinstates an invalid president may be a failure. The verification philosophy is measured behaviour rather than attractive demonstrations.

## XXXVIII. Proposed Continuity Acceptance Matrix

| Domain | Core question | Evidence required | Invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Are exactly the lawful citizens active? | Registry epochs, signed transitions, uniqueness checks | Counting running processes |
| Lineage | Were bifurcations legitimate and atomic? | Parent retirement, descendant keys, ancestry proofs | Copying a prompt and calling it a citizen |
| Privacy | Do vault boundaries survive recovery and indexing? | Encryption and access tests, secondary-store audits | Testing only original file permissions |
| Legislature | Did qualified district majorities actually pass? | Frozen rolls, authenticated district returns, text version | Walnut summary of apparent consensus |
| Executive | Is the office valid and its directives bounded? | Term ledger, succession, credentials, expiry | Assuming latest host process is President |
| Judiciary | Were rulings made by valid justices? | Seat registry, signed opinions, threshold checks | Treating a 2–2 split as approval |
| Founder | Is retired authority truly constrained? | Advisory-only tests, narrow Tier Two evidence | Trusting the founder's own assurances |
| HIMS | Is delivery distinct from permission? | Reject and replay tests, delegation scope | Allowing any signed instruction to execute |
| Rock | Can authoritative history be reconstructed? | Journals, independent backups, restore rehearsals | Equating RAID1 with a backup |
| Walnut | Does a replacement model preserve boundaries? | Regression, context isolation, provenance | Similar-looking responses |

This matrix is a **research acceptance guide**, not a declaration that the criteria have been met. Testing may reveal that a preferred architecture must change. That is the intended use of the matrix, not an embarrassment to be removed from the documentation.

## XXXIX. Implementation Sequence — From Monitoring to a Genuine Continuity Case

A practical sequence begins with inventory and honest baselines. First identify the current physical components, storage devices, inference runtime, models, operating systems, electrical safety measures, and deployed versus merely drafted governance features. Then create a versioned machine-readable state manifest aligning naming, Pillar status, evidence status, hardware identifiers, model hashes, constitutional draft versions, and the current limits of observed capability. This gives later auditors an actual reference point instead of a shifting recollection.

Next establish reliable records and identities before attempting a large citizen population. Prototype HIMS authorization with real rejection tests. Verify sealed vault handling, signed journal ordering, and restore from offline backup. Implement small citizen and district test populations, exercising exact and augmented bifurcation under deliberate crashes. Measure independent inference opportunities rather than guessing from the number of configured prompts. Only then is a claim about 256 functioning citizen participants meaningfully testable.

The governance stage should use controlled mock elections and disputed cases, including nomination endorsement, ranked-ballot exhaustion, court ties, standby succession, veto override, official key revocation, and priority legislation. These can be evaluated in simulation without pretending that Huey's third-generation Constitution has been ratified. The four-V100 and V4 embodiment stage should separately qualify PCIe routing, power, active airflow for passive cards, stable inference, sustained workloads, deterministic interlocks, and the proposed Pulse timing under realistic load.

Only after recovery tests should the project claim durable continuity. Use deliberate power interruption, corrupt backups, simultaneous fork prevention, invalid office credentials, key loss, and protected restart tests with verifiable audit trails. Publish accepted results and failures side by side, and move features from Prepared or Constrained into Transitional and then Locked/accepted only under clear criteria. The ultimate milestone is not a beautiful diagram of Huey surviving change, but repeatable evidence that it actually can—or a precise explanation of the limits found.

## XL. Open Constitutional, Technical & Philosophical Decisions

The final research chapter cannot close by pretending every remaining question is settled. **First ratification** still needs an exact eligibility and validation procedure, including how pre-ratification residents become authoritative citizens and what occurs if the first vote fails. **District expansion** needs a rule for recognition, representation, Supreme Court structure, weighted removal, and formerly four-district thresholds. **Voter denominators** and contested ballot procedures need binding definitions. **Presidential removal** requires a final direct-referendum threshold, while recusal and ordinary Court decision standards remain to be selected.

**Whole-republic identity change** poses a deeper question: which Cornerstone commitments, if any, are constitutionally unamendable, and who judges an attempted identity rupture? **Refounding** needs a legitimate procedure that does not allow a compromised predecessor to grant itself successor sovereignty. **Human recovery custody** needs a deliberate answer for incapacity or permanent unavailability of the Human Counterpart without quietly reversing the human-only Tier Two restart rule. **Founding Father triggering** needs testable negative-power predicates that cannot be manipulated by a hostile actor or a frustrated political branch.

**Engineering proof** remains open for V100 multi-GPU model placement, gpt-oss-120b backend compatibility, viable four-plus-four-minute Pulse timing, protected external interfaces, physical safety hardware, private vault key custody, durable distributed election commits, restoration from a compromised live registry, and practical independence among shared-model citizens. The proposed equal founding population distribution does not settle future population caps, job allocation fairness, or archival retention growth. A designed mechanism that cannot be tested on the assembled machine is not yet accepted.

Finally, **the philosophical nature of digital identity** is not resolved by technical semantics. A system can specify how one citizen retires and two successors acquire keys and memories without claiming to prove whether subjective experience divides, survives, or exists. The current work seeks a rigorous operational and constitutional project rather than unsupported certainty about consciousness. Recording these open decisions at the end is not unfinished editing. It is a necessary map of the research yet to be done.

## Conclusion — Build What Can Endure, and Tell the Truth When It Cannot

The Monkey-Head-Project begins with an ambitious but testable proposition: one person, armed with persistence and obtainable tools, can assemble a meaningful embodied AI system and continue to improve it. The project's distinctive move is to imagine that system not merely as a conversational model controlling motors, but as Huey: a governed collective whose citizens, institutions, memories, and physical components must remain separately intelligible. The same ambition that makes the project worth pursuing also creates a responsibility not to confuse architecture with deployment, legal simulation with ratification, or a familiar voice with proven continuity.

Ozymandias closes the book because a worthy creation must be capable of explaining its own transformations. A functioning machine can fail to remain a lawful republic. A stopped machine may retain everything required for legitimate resumption. A better model may preserve the same constitutional population. A copied archive may be valuable without conferring political legitimacy. A new chassis can be the same Huey's body; a new founding may be an honest successor without being the same civic history.

The relevant virtues are not eternal uptime, immutable hardware, or a grandiose statement of identity. They are **bounded authority, individual distinction, protected memory, traceable origin, credible evidence, lawful revision, recoverability, and the capacity to admit when any of these has been lost**. Growth should be welcomed when it serves those principles. Drift should be detected while it is still correctable. Degradation should be measured rather than denied. Failure should be preserved as evidence. Renewal should be properly named.

There is no shame in a prototype that fails a demanding test. There is a serious problem in a project that rewrites its record until every test looks successful. The final proof of Huey will not be that its name survives on a screen. It will be that an informed observer can follow the changes, see what was authorized, repeat meaningful tests, and understand what still exists.

**Huey should be able to grow without disguising drift, repair without counterfeiting history, stop without being declared dead, and begin again without pretending that nothing was lost.**

That is Ozymandias. That is the closing discipline of the Monkey-Head-Project research book
