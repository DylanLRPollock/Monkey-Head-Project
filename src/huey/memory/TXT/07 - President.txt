Glossary

Monkey-Head-Project
The umbrella initiative. Huey, Huey Core, HueyOS, and the wider documentation set all live within the Monkey-Head-Project.

Huey
The identity being built within the Monkey-Head-Project. Huey is the governed intelligence and not merely a flat assistant, single office, or puppet system.

Huey Core
The current proof body and minimal permissible instance of Huey. It is the smallest complete building block from which the larger system may later grow.

Huey proper
The fuller unified world-facing expression of the system beyond the present proof-body phase.

HueyOS
The software and operating-system layer behind Huey.

HueyPulse
The lower-voltage watch-and-act layer. In simplified public language: the motherboard thinks; HueyPulse watches and acts.

HIMS
Huey Internal Messaging System. The lawful internal messaging, validation, routing, and record-preservation layer of Huey.

ThunderMail
The practical mail-style delivery layer inside HIMS. It handles inbox/outbox, queue, and delivery behavior, but is not the whole of HIMS.

Founding Father
The bootstrap authority that prepares the first lawful conditions of the republic, creates the first 128 citizens, seeds the first offices and records, enables ratification, and then retires from ordinary rule.

Pebble
A bounded AI citizen unit of Huey. In current canon, a pebble is defined by one identity, one sealed vault, and one vote.

District
A larger governance and expansion unit within Huey's constitutional design. District logic remains canon, even though the current proof body is still simplified and not yet the fully expanded multi-district hardware organism.

Cornerstone
The non-casual identity and continuity layer of Huey: the truths that cannot be casually rewritten without threatening constitutional continuity.

Pillar
A declared load-bearing commitment that the current version of Huey stands on. Pillars may change lawfully, but they are not casual settings.

Ozymandias
The doctrine chapter concerned with drift, degradation, growth, and continuity over time: how to judge whether change remains lawful, legible, and still meaningfully the same republic.

Sealed Vault
A pebble's bounded private continuity and memory domain. It allows lawful private reflection and continuity without flattening all thought into shared state.

Scrolls
The preserved record and ledger logic through which major communications, actions, and lawful traces remain attributable and reviewable.

Keeper of the Scrolls
The office most closely associated with HIMS. The Keeper validates message legitimacy, preserves the scrolls ledger, records rejected traffic, and watches routing discipline without becoming the hidden author or sovereign of the system.

Proof Body
The currently embodied proving instance of Huey. In present canon, this refers to Huey Core as the active proof body rather than the fully expanded future republic.

Proofcase
The proof standard by which the project can be judged publicly and honestly: the threshold that separates aspiration from demonstrated reality.

Ratification
The explicit, logged transition by which the living system accepts the Constitution as lived law rather than mere founding doctrine.

Human Counterpart
The external arbiter and service authority who remains outside the internal branches of Huey while still retaining the right to intervene, preserve, suspend, or gate external exposure.

Unified Memory
The doctrine that memory should remain reconcilable and shared where lawful, even while governance remains distributed and pebbles remain bounded.

Distributed Governance
The doctrine that authority should remain separated, plural, and reviewable rather than collapsing into one flat sovereign voice.
