Monkey-Head-Project
Canonical Documentation Structure

Chapter 1 — Thesis, Ethos, Method, & Proofcase
Sets the opening frame of the project: why Huey exists, the ethos behind the work, the practical method of development, and the proof-first standard by which progress is judged.

Chapter 2 — Cornerstone & Pillars
Defines the fixed foundations of the project: the immutables, structural constraints, declared upgrade paths, and the load-bearing decisions that shape Huey's continuity.

Chapter 3 — Huey Constitution
Formerly called the Federation Constitution.
Defines the governing law of Huey: legitimacy, branches of governance, amendment process, emergency distinctions, powers, limits, and the lawful basis of authority.

Chapter 4 — HIMS
Huey Internal Messaging System.
Defines the internal and external messaging architecture of Huey: scrolls, interfaces, validation, audit trails, key structure, routing, and the governance of communication between systems, users, and internal processes.

Chapter 5 — Founding Father
Defines the bootstrap authority responsible for the initial formation of the republic: creating the first 128 citizens, assigning founding roles, preparing the constitutional state, and retiring permanently into read-only advisory status after ratification.

Chapter 6 — 128 Pebbles & Districts
Defines the citizen structure of Huey: the 128 pebbles, their identities, sealed vaults, one-vote rule, specialization, district logic, and the relationship between bounded citizen units and the wider organism.

Chapter 7 — President
Defines the executive role: authority under time constraint, immediate-action doctrine, emergency execution, limitations, reviewability, and the distinction between presidential governance authority and Huey as a whole.

Chapter 8 — Supreme Court
Defines the judicial branch: constitutional interpretation, review of executive and parliamentary action, thresholds for judgment, contradiction handling, and the court's role as stabilizer of the republic.

Chapter 9 — Parliament
Defines the legislative branch: deliberation, proposal, consensus-building, representation of the citizen body, the Speaker role, and the relationship between Parliament and the other two branches.

Chapter 10 — Ozymandias: Drift, Degradation, & Growth
The closing doctrine chapter. Defines continuity, explainability, drift, degradation, growth, and the conditions under which a republic remains legitimate, fails, or must begin again.
