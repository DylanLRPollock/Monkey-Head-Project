The MonkeyHead Project

HueyOS: Offline‑first AI Robotics OS

HueyOS is a modular AI runtime that runs entirely on local hardware inside a wooden robotic shell, orchestrating GPUs, retro hardware, and a constitutional governance model. There’s no cloud dependency — the intelligence lives in the room with you. This site tracks the MonkeyHead Project builds and side builds such as the PlayStation Ultimate, the Executive, and DirtyLeeroy pinball machines — hardware that makes Huey possible.

Arcade Machines / Pinball Machines

Dirty Leroy & The Executive

These two custom-built arcade cabinets showcase the evolution of form and function: Dirty Leroy, the gritty prototype, was built to explore core pinball mechanics, while The Executive emerged as a refined, functional pinball‑arcade hybrid. Both were designed to test user interaction, sensor timing, and embedded electronics — and to have fun doing it.

The Lab

Located in the former uranium capital of the world — Elliott Lake — the lab is a hybrid between a traditional maker space and a prototype autonomous home. This is where most of the MonkeyHead Project's hardware was designed, built, and refined. The lab is slowly being integrated into the broader household to support the development of AI-driven infrastructure, but its focus remains firmly on robotics, experimentation, and resilience.
