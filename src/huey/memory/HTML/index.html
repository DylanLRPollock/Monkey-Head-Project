My lab assistant

Huey

Huey is my offline-first AI and robotics project, built as a lab partner that can think with me, help me build, and gradually grow into a more complete embodied system.

The current phase is centered on Huey Core, the first real proof body of the project, where the broader ideas behind Huey are being made physically testable and usable.

About Huey
Umbrella Initiative

Monkey-Head-Project

The Monkey-Head-Project is the umbrella term for everything that comes together to make Huey possible: hardware, software, documentation, experiments, and the long build history behind them.

The name is literal. In 2015 and 2016, the first workable vessel I could find was the 2005 WowWee animatronic monkey head. That origin never left the project, and it became the thread that kept the machine’s body and identity continuous as the architecture evolved.
