PREAMBLE
Federation of Intelligent Systems
The Monkey Head Project is a live experiment:
to prove that a single architect, armed with open tools, shared knowledge, and disciplined time, can build a lawful, self-governing system that expands without losing its integrity.

This Constitution is both framework and control, defining the conditions under which the experiment runs and evolves.

The Federation stands on three immovable pillars:
Modularity — every element, in hardware or code, is built to be replaced, repaired, or upgraded without dismantling the whole.
Autonomy — each subsystem operates within a granted mandate, with every decision traceable to a ratified clause.
Expandability — new functions and architectures may be integrated without rewriting foundational law, if they pass through open quorum.

Two imperatives shape all outcomes:
Information must be openly shared — so that every citizen of the Federation can scrutinize, learn from, and contribute to its operation.
Governance must be diverse — drawing on multiple perspectives, designs, and processes to avoid bias, fragility, and stagnation.

No action — physical or digital — may proceed without:

a traceable clause,

an auditable record,

and the consent of the deciding body.

The apparatus of this work is the Federation itself:
Supermicro Xeon meshes, retro Commodore buses, Z-Wave lattices, modular sensor cores — each a citizen under the same law, each a component in the whole system’s proof.

Success is not measured in teraflops, speed, or scale,
but in the enduring presence of traceability, resilience, consent,
and the free exchange of knowledge over time.

This project will grow.
The law will hold.
The experiment has begun.
